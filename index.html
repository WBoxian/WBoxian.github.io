Personal project · Built independently11/2025 — 05/2026

Qilin QILIN

Search & Recommendation System

Built an integrated search and recommendation system using approximately 2 million notes and data from 15,000 users, covering preprocessing, feature engineering, retrieval, ranking, deployment and serving.

2Mnotes
15Kusers in dataset
150Ksearch & recommendation samples
~150msonline serving latency
  1. 01Data processing
  2. 02Features
  3. 03Retrieval
  4. 04Ranking
  5. 05Serving

Implementation

  • Retrieval: DSSM dual tower + Faiss, combined with Swing and UserCF.
  • Ranking: LambdaMART (LightGBM / XGBoost) for coarse ranking; DIEN for sequence-aware final ranking.
  • Serving: FastAPI + Redis, with real-time behavior writeback and linked search/recommendation.

Research experience

06/2024 — 12/2024

Environmental DNA assessment of coral communities

Zoological Society of London · ZSL

Analysed seawater environmental DNA from the Chagos Archipelago, combining it with CoralNet-assisted image annotation to assess coral composition across lagoon and exposed habitats.

Detected 5 additional scleractinian genera missed by conventional surveys; published as first and corresponding author in Environmental DNA.
09/2022 — 05/2023

Comparative transcriptomics of deep-sea mussels

Southern Marine Science and Engineering Guangdong Laboratory (Zhuhai)

Studied tissue differences in mussels from cold seeps and hydrothermal vents, performing RNA extraction, primer design, qRT-PCR, bioinformatics analysis and validation.

Identified elevated expression of host-defence genes in gill tissue; published as second author in Journal of Oceanology and Limnology.
